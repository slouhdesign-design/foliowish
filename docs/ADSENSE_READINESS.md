# AdSense Readiness

AdSense is the first monetization target, but ads are deliberately absent from pre-launch.

## Before application
- Public domain is owned and stable.
- Search engines can crawl the approved public pages.
- About, Privacy, Terms and Contact are final and identify the operator as required.
- The site contains a meaningful footprint of original, useful content rather than empty tool pages.
- Navigation, mobile UX, accessibility and page speed are clean.
- No copyrighted magazine branding, copied template designs, fake testimonials, fake reviews or fake traffic.
- Consent/advertising disclosure is configured for applicable regions.

## Ad placement policy for FolioWish
- Never put an ad inside the magazine canvas or export output.
- Never place ads so close to editor controls that accidental clicks are likely.
- Do not interrupt photo upload, Smart Fill or PDF export with interstitial-style friction.
- Prefer editorial pages and non-destructive placements around the tool.
- User trust and repeat use outrank short-term ad density.

## Growth requirement
We apply only when the public site has enough real content and traffic signals to justify review. Approval and revenue are never guaranteed.
