# Domain plan

## Planned public domain
`foliowish.com`

- Availability was checked on 2026-08-30 and it was available at that time.
- The domain is not purchased or connected yet.
- Do not publish canonical tags pointing to it until the domain is owned and DNS is under our control.
- `.com` is preferred because the product is English-language and globally targeted.
- The name has no hyphen, number, location lock-in or keyword stuffing.

## Launch rule
The public origin is supplied at launch through `PUBLIC_ORIGIN`. The unlock script refuses to open indexing unless `ALLOW_PUBLIC_LAUNCH=YES` is also set.
