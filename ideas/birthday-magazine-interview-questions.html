Interview prompts

75 Birthday Magazine Interview Questions People Actually Enjoy

The best interview questions are easy enough to answer quickly but specific enough to reveal personality. Mix short fun questions with a few reflective prompts instead of turning the birthday magazine into a job interview.

Home / Ideas / Interview prompts

Quick personality questions

What is your current comfort food?; What song do you never skip?; What tiny purchase improves your day?; What is your most-used emoji?; What smell instantly takes you somewhere else?; What is your default coffee or drink order?; What could you talk about for twenty minutes with no preparation?; What outfit always feels like you?; What app do you open too often?; What is a hill you will defend forever?; What is your most specific pet peeve?; What is the last thing that made you laugh properly?; What fictional home would you move into?; What is your ideal slow morning?; What snack disappears fastest around you?

Friendship questions

What makes you feel cared for?; What kind of friend do you try to be?; What is a tiny memory with friends you still think about?; Who can always tell when something is wrong?; What is your favorite kind of plan: scheduled, spontaneous or cancelled?; What is the funniest misunderstanding you remember?; What friendship habit do you want to keep forever?; What makes someone easy to trust?; What group tradition deserves to survive?; What is one thing your friends know not to let you do alone?; What makes a great road-trip person?; What compliment from a friend stayed with you?; What is one thing you appreciate more as you get older?; What is your favorite way to celebrate someone else?; What do you hope your friends remember about this era?

Reflective questions

What are you proud of from the last year?; What did you learn the slow way?; What are you getting better at saying no to?; What are you learning to ask for?; What version of yourself would surprise younger you?; What feels easier now than it did a year ago?; What still feels difficult but worth doing?; What have you changed your mind about?; What ordinary thing are you grateful for?; What is something you no longer need to prove?; What do you want to protect more carefully?; What do you want more time for?; What has made you feel most like yourself lately?; What mistake taught you something useful?; What does a good life look like this month, not someday?

Future-facing questions

Where do you want to wake up on a random Tuesday next year?; What is one skill you want to learn?; What trip would you say yes to immediately?; What project do you want to finish?; What habit would make daily life easier?; Who do you want to spend more time with?; What are you curious about right now?; What is one risk that might be worth taking?; What do you want future-you to thank you for?; What would make this year feel memorable?; What kind of work would make you proud?; What do you want to own less of?; What do you want to experience more of?; What should definitely be on the next birthday playlist?; What promise do you want to make to yourself?

Funny questions

What would your warning label say?; What is your most chaotic talent?; Which minor inconvenience becomes a personal crisis?; What would you be famous for in an alternate universe?; If your life had a recurring sound effect, what would it be?; What is the worst fashion choice you would defend anyway?; Which food opinion could start an argument?; What is your most unnecessary skill?; If your camera roll had a theme, what would it be?; What is the most “you” way to be late?; What reality show would eliminate you first?; What object do you lose constantly?; What phrase do you say too much?; What is your most suspiciously specific expertise?; Which friend would survive a ridiculous emergency best?
