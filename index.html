Questions

Before you start

Is the birthday magazine maker really free?

Yes. The current maker works without an account and does not require a paid template. Future optional features may be separate, but the core editor and project export are designed to stay useful on their own.

Do my photos get uploaded?

In this pre-launch version, photo processing happens in your browser and the project is stored locally. Do not clear site storage until you have exported a backup project file.

Can I save the magazine as a PDF?

Use the Export PDF button. FolioWish prepares all pages in print layout and opens your browser's print dialog; choose “Save as PDF” for a digital file.

What paper size does it use?

The first production format is A4 portrait. This keeps the editor simple and predictable. US Letter is on the roadmap and can be added before public launch if testing shows enough demand.

Can I use FolioWish on a phone?

The editor is responsive and can be used on mobile, but magazine work is much easier on a laptop or tablet because you are reviewing full pages and photos.
